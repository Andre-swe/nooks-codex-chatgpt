# Security

## Report a vulnerability

Email hello@nooks.place with **security** in the subject line. Do not open a
public issue for a vulnerability.

## Scope

In scope: the files in this repository, and the public Nooks endpoints they
configure or link to, including `https://nooks.place/api/mcp-v2` and
`https://nooks.place/api/mcp`.

## Rules

- No bug bounty is offered or promised.
- Do not include secrets, access tokens, personal data or another person's build
  content in a report. If a credential is involved, say where it was found and
  use a placeholder.
- Test only what you are authorised to test. Do not access another person's
  builds or data.
- Give us a reasonable chance to fix the issue before writing about it publicly.

## What we can share

We can confirm what was fixed and when a fix is public. We do not publish
internal details of the hosted service.
