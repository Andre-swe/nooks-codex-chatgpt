# Research and Nooks' adaptation

[BrickBench: Evaluating Agentic Brick Design](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.12452v1),
sections 4.2–4.4, 5.2 and 5.4, separates construction validity, prompt alignment
and design quality. Semantic questions have dependencies. The construction
environment provides connector-aware tools and interpretable checks. Evaluation
uses multiple views and pairwise comparisons; reversing candidate order reduces
position bias. The tool ablation supports grounded verification, while the human
comparison shows that satisfying requirements still leaves a design gap.
The paper does not establish a minimum number of improvement cycles or evaluate
this plugin or Brain Flakes.

Nooks applies those ideas through source-photo preservation, visible-feature
records, consistent cameras, targeted changes and retaining the best valid
revision. Its seven-feature photo review and draft states are product choices.
Their AI-inferred results are not benchmark scores, human review or physical
tests.
